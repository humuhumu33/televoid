## The story so far

Someone once wired a video model that dreams faster than you can watch into a
live stream. Viewers typed whatever crossed their minds; the machine turned it
into television — scene after scene, forever, never repeating. Actual
interdimensional cable. The internet loved it.

The platform banned it within days.

That is the oldest rule of broadcasting: when your channel lives on someone
else's server, it is theirs to switch off. TELEVOID is the same wonderful
idea, rebuilt so that **there is no switch**.

## What you are looking at

A television. Not a website that plays videos — a set that RECEIVES.

Somewhere, an operator runs a broadcast desk. You type a transmission into
your set — *a couch that reviews couches*, *a weather report for a planet
made of soup* — and the studio dreams it into an actual broadcast. When your
clip airs, your words appear on the lower third, on every screen, in every
country, at the same instant. Everyone watching a channel sees the same
moment together. That is the whole magic: it is LIVE, and it is yours.

Between realities there is static. There is always static. Press **ZAP** —
the button, the spacebar, or just tap the screen — and surf the realities
your set has already caught. The red light means you are back on the live
moment with everyone else.

## How it stays alive

**100% serverless.** There is no broadcast tower, no data center, no company
in the middle. Instead:

* **Your set is part of the station.** The moment you start watching, your
  set quietly passes the signal along to the sets that arrive after you.
  You do nothing. There is no toggle. More viewers means a STRONGER channel,
  not a bigger bill — an audience that grows its own transmitter.
* **Watching is remembering.** Every set keeps a little of what it saw, so a
  newcomer tunes into the last few realities instantly — served by the
  audience, not by anyone's machine in particular. The desk can vanish
  entirely and the channel's memory stays alive in the sets that watched.
* **A channel is a key, not an account.** Nothing to suspend, nothing to
  seize, no terms of service between you and the picture. Block one meeting
  point and the sets simply meet somewhere else.
* **Waiting is weather.** The set never says loading. When something takes a
  moment, you get static — because static is a picture, and a set tuning
  between realities is exactly what you are.

## Tune in

1. **[Open the page](https://humuhumu33.github.io/televoid/)** and press the
   power button — you get a fresh broadcast desk with its own channel.
2. Paste a [fal.ai](https://fal.ai) key into the transmitter power field and
   viewer transmissions become real dreamed video. Leave it empty and the
   desk airs its built in test pattern — the network works either way.
3. Press **beam this channel** and send the link anywhere. Anyone who opens
   it is watching your live moment — and strengthening your station.
